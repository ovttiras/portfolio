About Me

AI/ML Engineer, PhD in Chemistry and Associate Professor who applies best practices to build products used by scientists and businesses. Now in fintech, I develop enterprise AI solutions with local LLMs: a RAG assistant for the knowledge base, OCR validation of passport data, call-center speech analytics, and an AI agent for code review and software development.

I defended my PhD on applying machine learning methods to predict the toxicity of chemical compounds in 2017, and I remain active in research: more than 30 scientific publications, 40 reviews for scientific journals, and 7 web applications on AI and machine learning for drug discovery.
